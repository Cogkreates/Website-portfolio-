Unicoursity

Live

Every accredited course at every Nigerian university, in one place.

Built on the NUC's 2023 CCMAS dataset — around 4,079 courses across the country — so prospective students can browse what's actually accredited. Ships as a single HTML app on Vercel with Supabase handling auth and data, plus a CGPA calculator and course bookmarking.

Subkill

In Progress

An agent that watches your inbox so subscriptions stop watching your wallet.

Syncs with Gmail to spot subscriptions and free trials, then tracks their renewal dates. Before a charge lands, it asks whether you still want the service — no reply, and it pauses the subscription on its own. Subscriptions you trust can opt into virtual-card protection instead. In development for the "Agents for Humans" hackathon, built on AWS Strands Agents.
